Target Audience 
The Cisco Networking Academy® CCNP Enterprise curriculum is designed for participants who 
are seeking professional-level jobs in the ICT industry or hope to fulfill prerequisites to pursue 
other CCNP or CCIE certifications. The entire curriculum is appropriate for learners at 
universities and colleges. 
Prerequisites 
CCNA or equivalent knowledge and skills. 
Course Description 
In this curriculum, Cisco Networking Academy™ participants learn, apply, and practice CCNP 
Enterprise knowledge and skills through a series of in-depth, hands-on experiences that 
reinforce their learning.  
The CCNP Enterprise v9 curriculum is presented in two courses that provide integrated and 
comprehensive coverage of professional-level networking technologies.  
Upon completion of each course, learners will be prepared to take the certification exam 
associated with that course. 
The following two courses comprise the CCNP Enterprise v9 curriculum: 
CCNP Enterprise: Core Networking v9 
The Implementing and Operating Cisco Enterprise Network Core Technologies (ENCOR) course 
provides learners with the knowledge and skills needed to install, configure, operate, and 
troubleshoot an enterprise network. This course introduces overlay network design through 
SD-Access and SD-WAN solutions and teaches learners to implement security principles and 
automation and programmability within an enterprise network. This course helps prepare 
learners to take the 350-401 ENCOR exam, which is part of the CCIE Enterprise Infrastructure 
certification. This version is aligned with the updated version of the 350-401 exam, version 
1.2. 
Learners will be able to: 
• Configure and implement identified solutions by applying planned implementation 
processes using Cisco IOS Software commands and applications. 
• Verify appropriate show and debug commands and applications to ensure correct 
solution implementation and performance. 
• Troubleshoot appropriate show and debug commands and applications to identify the 
cause of basic-level network issues and correctly implement a solution that ensures the 
network is performing as desired. 
CCNP Enterprise: Advanced Routing v9 
The Implementing Cisco Enterprise Advanced Routing and Services (ENARSI) course provides 
learners with the knowledge and skills needed to install, operate, manage, and troubleshoot an 
enterprise network. Throughout the course, learners will build the skills necessary to ensure the 
smooth and effective operation of these networks. Completing this course prepares them to 
take the Implementing Cisco Enterprise Advanced Routing and Services (300-410 ENARSI)

Course Features 
The CCNP Enterprise v9 curriculum includes many features to engage and assess learner 
knowledge and skills. 
Curriculum 
The online curriculum has been repurposed for use by the Cisco Networking Academy from the 
premium offerings at Cisco U. 
The Cisco U. built-in, hands-on labs, (using the terminology “Discovery”), available to Cisco U. 
premium subscribers, have been stripped and are only available at Cisco Networking Academy 
as video-based demonstrations. 
The course at Cisco Networking Academy has been expanded with additional hands-on 
practice activities that leverage the Cisco Hardware Classroom Equipment, Cisco Packet 
Tracer, or Cisco Modeling Labs free edition (CML-Free). These lab activities are threaded 
throughout the course using the Assignment Flow. 
Assessments 
Assessments and practice activities are focused on specific certification competencies to 
increase knowledge and skill retention. 
Embedded module-group assessments provide immediate feedback to support the evaluation 
of knowledge and acquired skills.

Activities 
Hands-on labs help students develop critical thinking and complex, problem-solving skills. 
It is recommended to leverage CML-Free to emulate real network operating systems and 
practice important network virtualization skills and concepts, such as a digital twin. 
Cisco Packet Tracer simulation-based learning activities are added as optional instructor 
resources to support vital CCNA skills. 
Video demonstrations give insight into complex workflows and processes providing exposure to 
advanced networking tools and operations.
